# Gruff Editor

The first multicam edit of your podcast, done for you.

Give it your episode's camera files and mic tracks. Gruff lines them up,
works out who is talking, and hands you a finished multicam edit to open
in Adobe Premiere Pro.

## Download

**[Download Gruff Editor for Windows](https://github.com/mikecaropreso/gruff-editor-releases/releases/latest/download/Gruff.Editor.Setup.exe)** (1.3 GB, free while in testing)

Already have it? It checks for updates by itself once a day.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11
- Adobe Premiere Pro
- A camera per person, plus a wide camera if you have one
- A separate mic track per person (one WAV each)
- An NVIDIA graphics card makes transcribing much faster (optional)

## The first time you open the installer

Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box. Gruff Editor is
signed, but it is new, so Windows has not seen many installs yet. Click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Something wrong?

In the app, click **Make a report to send**. It comes straight to us.

Questions, ideas, or just to say how it went: [join the Gruff Editor Discord](https://discord.gg/qnZQd9Fpj4).
